# Publication safeguards

Every new page must answer a distinct user need with original useful detail. Add it to the typed registry only after checking factual claims and source support. Do not publish placeholders, repetitive split/day-count pages, generated keyword variants, hidden text, fabricated reviews or search-only copy.

Use primary research, professional position statements and authoritative documentation. Verify links and the actual claim they support. Explain the studied population and limitations. Do not imply diagnosis, medical treatment or guaranteed outcomes. No supplement articles without a separate evidence/safety assessment.

Current attribution is the publisher Fitician, not an invented expert. No independent clinical/coach review has been performed or claimed. A future author/reviewer must have a real public identity, consent, relevant credentials verified by the owner, recorded review and corrections responsibility. Product specialist status alone grants no editorial authorship.

Publication dates reflect first publication of these texts; dates in the source registry are not deployment timestamps. Record updated/reviewed dates only after an actual material edit/review. Never refresh dates just to appear recent. Sitemaps deliberately omit lastmod until publication history is recorded accurately.

AI assistance does not remove accountability. Check every citation and numerical claim. Human scientific review is a high-impact follow-up before expanding health content. Use `/support` for corrections and record material corrections in content history.

Public exercises are build projections of explicitly approved, reviewed educational records. The exporter permits only public names, taxonomy, execution, safety and approved media presentation fields. Engine metadata, review/source identifiers, admin fields and personal data are excluded. Imported placeholder review instructions cannot be exported. Missing dedicated safety notes are clearly labeled with general guidance rather than fabricated exercise-specific review. Media publication requires explicit rights and verified public availability. Publication uses an explicit release snapshot, rights checks and deactivation handling; active status alone is insufficient.

Keep one canonical URL per subject; public routes must not overlap member URLs. Use native public anchors and breadcrumbs. Link every new page from a useful hub and related pages. Do not publish empty filters/tag pages. Keep sitemap URLs canonical and indexable; removed URLs must return 404/410 or a relevant permanent redirect.
